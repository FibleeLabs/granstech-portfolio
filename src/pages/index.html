01 Company overview

Engineered for geometric and metallurgical precision.

A steel foundry specialising in machined castings in complex geometries and demanding material grades, with a dedicated quick-ship capability. Our plant sits in the Coimbatore industrial belt with road, rail, air and seaport connectivity for domestic and export orders to more than 10 countries.

About GransTech

Machined castings

Fully machined castings in complex geometries and tight tolerances, with quick-ship capability.

250 MT installed capacity per month

Supports recurring production lots and rapid prototype delivery across CO2 and Airset moulding lines.

Up to 1,000 kg unit weight

Raw single-piece castings from lightweight valve bodies up to 1,000 kg (2,204 lbs).

Multi-process moulding

CO2 process, Airset process, Shell process* and Investment casting process*.

* Supported through qualified vendor partners.

380 KVA power backup

Uninterrupted melting, heat treatment and finishing operations.

Coimbatore industrial hub

South India's manufacturing corridor, 35 km from Coimbatore International Airport and about 350 km from Tuticorin and Cochin ports.
