02 Chemical & mechanical labs

Verified in-house before every release.

Spectro analysis lab

Spectro analyser

SPECTROMAXx Fe/Ni-based unit testing 29 elements with certified reference materials (CRM) for carbon, low alloy, stainless, duplex and manganese steel.

Mechanical lab

UTM & impact testing

Universal tensile testing machine and Charpy impact testing, capable of testing at cryogenic temperatures down to −196°C.

Hardness testing

Brinell & Rockwell hardness

Dual hardness stations for 100% verification of heat-treated casting lots against material grade standards.

Chemical lab

Wet analysis lab

Secondary validation of raw materials, alloys, slag and chemical solution concentrations.

Sand lab

Sieve analysis

Moulding sand grain distribution checked and logged to keep mould quality consistent.

Radiography

X-ray film reviewer

Darkroom film viewing for radiographic evaluation of volumetric defects.

03 Non-destructive examination

Documented NDE procedures.

Each method runs to a controlled work instruction, performed by qualified personnel.

Procedure WI-37 R1

Liquid penetrant (DP)

Detects fine surface discontinuities, cracks and pinholes on finished and machined surfaces.

Procedure WI-38 R1

Magnetic particle (MPI)

Electromagnetic yokes detect surface and near-surface flaws in ferromagnetic steel castings.

Procedure WI-39 R1

Ultrasonic (UT)

Internal volumetric flaw detection, wall thickness verification and sound velocity analysis.

Procedure WI-40 R1

Radiographic (RT)

Iridium-192 and Cobalt-60 isotope sources with darkroom film review for volumetric defect evaluation.

Procedure WI-47 R0

Visual inspection

100% visual inspection of raw and finished machined components by qualified inspectors before final release.

04 Traceability & documentation

Full traceability, from heat number to shipment.

Every casting is traceable to its sales order, heat and grade, and ships with complete inspection and export documentation.

  • Material test certificates (MTC) with chemical and mechanical results
  • Inspection and test certificates
  • Dimensional and NDT reports
  • Heat treatment records with T1 / T2 thermocouple logs
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Customer-specific documentation as required
