01 Who we are

Machined castings with complex geometries and demanding grades.

GransTech (formerly Anugraha Castings) is a precision steel foundry delivering high-quality machined castings for complex applications. With a monthly capacity of 250 MT and casting sizes up to 1,000 kg, we use CO2, Airset, Shell and Investment casting processes. Supported by robust infrastructure including 380 KVA power backup, we are headquartered in Coimbatore, India, supplying reliable, high-integrity components for critical industrial sectors.

  • High-tolerance casting. Fully machined parts in complex geometries.
  • Pressure-ready performance. PED, AD 2000, API 20A, IBR and NORSOK M650 approvals.
  • Flow-critical reliability. Valve and pump castings verified by chemical, mechanical and NDE testing.

07 Frequently asked

Answers to common sourcing questions.

Something else you need to know? Our technical sales team replies to every enquiry.

Ask a question

What types of castings does GransTech manufacture?

GransTech specialises in steel castings with complex geometries, including machined and near-net-shape components for critical applications.

What casting processes are supported?

We support multiple processes depending on design and application needs: CO2 / Airset moulding, Shell moulding* and Investment casting* (*supported with qualified vendor partners).

What materials and grades do you work with?

We regularly cast and machine carbon steel, low alloy steel, stainless steel, martensitic stainless steel, duplex stainless steel and nickel alloys.

What is the maximum casting weight you can supply?

GransTech can manufacture raw castings up to 1,000 kg (2,204 lbs) per piece.

Do you offer quick turnaround or urgent orders?

Yes. Our Quick Ship Program supports urgent requirements with lead times starting from 14 days, subject to pattern and material approval.

Do you manage and ship castings internationally?

Yes. GransTech supports global shipments and supplies cast components to customers across multiple international markets.

What is your monthly production capacity?

Our installed capacity is 250 metric tons per month, with capacity expansion underway as per our growth roadmap.

Do you support casting design and simulation?

Yes. We use MAGMASOFT and ProCAST simulation software to optimise gating, feeding and solidification during the early design stage.

Are machining and post-processing handled in-house?

Yes. We offer in-house or controlled partner support for heat treatment, shot blasting and fettling, welding (qualified WPS & PQR), pickling and passivation, and machining and dimensional inspection.

What quality and inspection systems are in place?

GransTech follows a stringent quality process including chemical and mechanical testing, spectro analysis, UT, RT, MPI and DP inspections, impact testing down to −196°C, and full traceability with inspection documentation.

What export documentation do you provide?

We provide complete export documentation, including commercial invoice and packing list, inspection and test certificates, material test certificates (MTC), dimensional and NDT reports, and customer-specific documentation as required.
