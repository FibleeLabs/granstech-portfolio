Process stages

Moulding to finishing, under one roof.

Stage 01 / Moulding

Core & mould making: resin sand / CO2 process

Every core and mould is inspected and logged against its sales order, heat and grade number. Coating Baume (60), mould hardness and pre-heat are recorded before the pour, giving accurate dimensions, sound internal geometries and repeatable results across production batches.

ProcessesCO2, Airset (resin sand); Shell* and Investment* through vendor partners
ControlsSieve analysis sand logs, Baume coat verification, hardness and pre-heat records
Pattern library1,000+ active patterns across valves, pumps and defence
Stage 02 / Melting

Induction melting & refining

The charge is formulated per the material data sheet. Chemistry is verified on the spectrometer before and after alloy additions, slag is removed and refined, and tapping temperature is confirmed with an immersion pyrometer for consistent composition, purity and temperature control.

Furnaces250 kW induction furnace with 300 kg and 500 kg crucibles
Expansion750 kW induction furnace with 1.5 MT crucible From Dec 2025
Roadmap3 MT and 8 MT crucibles 2026
ControlsSpectro analysis per heat, immersion pyrometer, controlled alloy additions
Stage 03 / Heat treatment

Bogie hearth heat treatment

Castings and test bars are heat-treated heat-wise and grade-wise to each grade's cycle. Temperatures are measured by two thermocouples (T1 and T2) and recorded in the heat treatment log for soak and quench control, improving strength, toughness and wear resistance while relieving internal stress.

Furnace3-ton HT furnace with tilting-type bogie
Quenching60,000 litre quench tank
CyclesAnnealing, normalising, quenching, tempering, stress relieving
Roadmap5-ton HT furnace with tilting bogie and 2-ton auto-quench facility Early 2026
Stage 04 / Post-process

Shot blasting, fettling & welding

A 500 kg shot blasting unit and eight heavy grinding stations prepare every casting for inspection. Repair welding follows qualified WPS and PQR, witnessed by TUV, for carbon steel, stainless steel group grades and Duplex grade 4A.

Shot blasting500 kg capacity; 3-ton unit planned as demand requires
Fettling8 heavy grinding stations
WeldingWPS & PQR available for carbon steel, stainless steel group grades and Duplex 4A, witnessed by TUV
Stage 05 / Finishing

Pickling & passivation

Stainless and duplex castings are acid pickled, passivated and rinsed in demineralised water, with 100% surface sign-off before release to machining and final inspection.

Pickling18–20% HNO3 + 3–5% HF, 2 hours
Passivation18–20% HNO3, 3 hours
Rinse & releaseDM water rinse; 100% surface sign-off

Technology

Casting simulation before the first pour.

Gating, feeding and solidification are optimised at the design stage, so new parts are right the first time.

  • MAGMASOFT and ProCAST, used depending on the need.
  • Supported by three strategic vendors located close to the facility.
  • Used for new product development and to optimise existing processes.
  • The outsourcing model gives quick simulation turnaround and the flexibility to use multiple software packages.
