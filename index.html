BATCH ROUTE / HEAT-TO-SHIP

Inside the Foundry Floor

From core & mould to heat treatment, fettling and finishing — every stage is logged, inspected and signed off before the next begins.

STAGE 01 / MOULDING

Moulding — Resin Sand / CO2 Process

Core and mould inspection logged against SO, heat and grade number — coating baume (60), hardness, pre-heat recorded before pour.

  • Sieve analysis sand logs
  • Baume coat verification
STAGE 02 / MELTING

Induction Melting & Refining

Formulated per data sheet. Chemistry verified by Spectrometer before/after alloy additions. Immersion pyrometer temp check.

  • 250KW Induction furnace
  • 750KW (1.5 MT from Dec 2025)
STAGE 03 / HEAT TREATMENT

Bogie Hearth Heat Treatment

Castings and test bars heat-treated per grade cycle with dual thermocouple (T1/T2) monitoring for soak and quench control.

  • 3-Ton HT furnace bogie
  • 60,000L Quenching Tank
STAGE 04 / POST-PROCESS

Shot Blasting & Welding

500 Kg shot blasting unit coupled with 8 heavy grinding stations. WPS & PQR repair welding witnessed by TÜV.

  • 8 Grinding stations
  • TÜV Witnessed Duplex 4A
STAGE 05 / FINISHING

Pickling & Passivation

Acid pickling (18-20% HNO3 + 3-5% HF, 2 hrs) followed by passivation (18-20% HNO3, 3 hrs) and DM water rinse.

  • Chemical dip tanks
  • 100% Surface signoff

QUALITY CONTROL & NDE CARDS

Chemical, Mechanical & Non-Destructive Examination

SPECTRO ANALYSIS LAB

SPECTRO ANALYSER

SPECTROMAXx Fe/Ni based unit testing 29 elements with certified reference materials (CRM) for Carbon, Low Alloy, Stainless, Duplex & Manganese Steel.

MECHANICAL LAB

UTM & IMPACT TESTING

Universal Tensile Testing Machine & Charpy Impact Testing setup capable of testing at cryogenic temperatures down to −196°C.

HARDNESS TESTING

BRINELL & ROCKWELL HARDNESS

Dual hardness testing stations for 100% verification of heat-treated casting lots against material grade standards.

CHEMICAL LAB

WET ANALYSIS LAB

Wet chemical verification lab for secondary validation of raw materials, alloys, slag, and chemical solution concentrations.

PROCEDURE WI-37 R1

LIQUID PENETRANT EXAMINATION (DP)

Dye Penetrant inspection procedure for detecting fine surface discontinuities, cracks, and pinholes on finished and machined surfaces.

PROCEDURE WI-38 R1

MAGNETIC PARTICLE EXAMINATION (MPI)

Magnetic Particle examination using electromagnetic yokes for detecting surface and near-surface flaws in ferromagnetic steel castings.

PROCEDURE WI-39 R1

ULTRASONIC EXAMINATION (UT)

High-frequency ultrasonic examination for internal volumetric flaw detection, wall thickness verification, and sound velocity analysis.

PROCEDURE WI-40 R1

RADIOGRAPHIC EXAMINATION (RT)

Radiographic examination using Iridium-192 and Cobalt-60 isotope sources with darkroom film reviewer for non-destructive volumetric defect evaluation.

PROCEDURE WI-47 R0

VISUAL INSPECTION OF COMPONENTS

100% Visual inspection of all raw and finished machined components by qualified inspector personnel prior to final release.
